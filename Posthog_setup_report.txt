Identification
Identification was intentionally skipped. The reviewed site is a marketing site with no application user, authentication, registration, session persistence, or logout boundary. Captures and automatically captured browser errors therefore remain anonymous by design. No DISTINCT_ID placeholders were introduced.

Follow-up issue — future identity boundary: If authentication or registration is added later, add posthog.identify at successful login/registration using a stable non-PII identifier and call posthog.reset at logout. Without this, authenticated returning activity will remain anonymous and cannot be reliably connected to a person.
